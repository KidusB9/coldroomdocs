ColdRoomDocs

Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

This policy covers the ColdRoomDocs Uploader, a private, single-user tool that the owner of the ColdRoomDocs YouTube channel runs on their own computer, and this website.

YouTube API Services

The ColdRoomDocs Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the tool accesses:

  • the authorized channel's ID, title and status, to confirm it is uploading to the correct channel;
  • the titles and IDs of the channel's recent uploads, to avoid uploading the same video twice;
  • the ability to upload videos to the channel and set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, privacy and scheduled publish times;
  • the processing status, privacy status and age-restriction status of videos it uploaded, to report problems to the owner.

It does not access viewers' data, comments, subscribers, analytics, payments, email or any other Google service.

What is stored, and where

Everything stays on the channel owner's own computer:

  • the OAuth access and refresh tokens, in a local file that is never shared or published;
  • a local log of uploads: video IDs, titles, upload and publish times, and status checks.

Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking.

Deleting data and revoking access

The owner can delete the local token and upload log at any time. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation the tool can no longer access the channel. Locally stored data is kept only as long as the owner keeps it, and stored API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days as YouTube's developer policies require.
