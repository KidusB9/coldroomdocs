ColdRoomDocs

Narrated true-crime documentaries built from public records: interrogation-room video, body-worn camera footage, 911 audio and court filings released by law enforcement agencies and courts in the United States.

Watch

The channel lives on YouTube: youtube.com/@ColdRoomDocs.

The ColdRoomDocs Uploader

The ColdRoomDocs Uploader is a private, single-user tool run by the channel owner on their own computer. It uses YouTube API Services to upload the channel's own finished videos to the ColdRoomDocs channel, together with their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and scheduled publish times. It uploads only to the owner's channel and is not offered to other users.

By using the tool, the owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How the tool handles data is described in the Privacy Policy; the rules for using it are in the Terms of Service.

Concerns about a video

If you appear in a video, or are the family member of a victim, and have a concern about how you or your loved one is shown, open a request at github.com/KidusB9/coldroomdocs/issues. Reasonable requests to blur or remove material are honored.
